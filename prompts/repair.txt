
Before revising the schedule:

1. Identify the root causes of the reported violations.

2. Reconstruct the chronological movement of each vessel
   from its initial starting port.

3. Reconstruct the movement of each cargo shipment
   from its origin to its final destination.

4. Ensure cargo is physically available at a port
   before assigning it to a departing vessel.

5. Do not create unnecessary, repeated, or circular
   voyages unless they are required for cargo delivery
   or repositioning.

6. Respect the maximum voyage count for each vessel.

7. Do not introduce additional voyages merely to
   address individual constraint violations.

8. Ensure all departures, arrivals, and cargo transfers
   are temporally consistent.

9. Remove unnecessary voyages from the previous plan.

10. Return a complete revised schedule that satisfies
    all original constraints.


STRICT OUTPUT FORMAT:

Return a JSON object with exactly one top-level key:
"voyages".

The output must begin with:
{"voyages": [

Do NOT wrap the output inside "revised_schedule",
"schedule", "result", or any other key.

IMPORTANT CARGO RULE:
A vessel travelling to a shipment's origin port to
collect cargo must travel empty unless other cargo
is already available at its departure port.

Cargo can only be loaded at a port where that cargo
is physically available at the departure time.
