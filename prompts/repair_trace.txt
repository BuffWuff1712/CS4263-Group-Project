SIMULATION TRACE:

The input includes simulation_trace, produced by the verifier replaying
previous_schedule. Use it instead of reconstructing movements yourself:

- vessels: each vessel's voyages in time order, with departure and arrival
  hours and the cargo on board.
- shipments: for each shipment, groups of container units that moved
  together, the legs they took, and their final status (delivered on time,
  delivered late, or not delivered and where they are waiting).

Use the trace to find where each problem starts, then change only what is
needed to fix it.
